1. The Rising Cost and Friction of Returns Processing

E-commerce return rates can exceed 25% in apparel and consumer electronics. Manually inspecting returned merchandise, categorizing return rationales, and identifying organized retail fraud (wardrobing, counterfeit substitutions) consumes extensive warehouse labor and delays restocking of high-velocity inventory.

2. The Risk of Hallucinations in Conversational Shopping Assistants

Deploying conversational AI on e-commerce storefronts carries severe brand risks if models hallucinate incorrect product dimensions, fabricate non-existent discounts, or promise delivery dates contrary to actual warehouse inventory. Retailers require mathematically grounded, deterministic catalog retrieval.
