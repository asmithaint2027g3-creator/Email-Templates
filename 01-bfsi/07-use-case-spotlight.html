Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and sanctions screening operations represent one of the heaviest operational burdens in commercial banking. Compliance teams review thousands of automated false-positive alerts daily, manually assembling transaction histories, entity ownership documents, and adverse media reports. TRUSTGRID.AI has engineered a deterministic agentic workflow to transform this process.

The Operational Challenge: Alert Fatigue and Manual Synthesis

Compliance analysts spend up to 75% of their working hours simply aggregating data from disparate internal databases, SWIFT messaging logs, and corporate registry filings before making a risk assessment. This operational drag delays suspicious activity report (SAR) filings and inflates operational compliance budgets.

The Proposed Multi-Agent Investigation Architecture

Under our proposed approach, when an alert triggers in the transaction monitoring system, a coordinated swarm of specialized agents initiates preliminary dossier compilation:

1. Entity Resolution Agent: Ingests structured transaction records and disambiguates counterparty entities against internal customer databases and corporate registries.
2. Dossier Synthesis Agent: Summarizes multi-year transaction patterns, flags anomalies against customer baseline behavior, and extracts relevant adverse media mentions.
3. Provenance Citation Engine: Every sentence in the generated case narrative contains a strict cryptographic link back to raw transaction hashes or verified registry documents.

Mandatory Human Decision Authority and Audit Readiness

The agent fleet does not close alerts or submit SAR filings autonomously. Instead, it presents a fully verified, citation-backed dossier directly to a senior compliance officer, who reviews the evidence, verifies citations, and makes the final regulatory determination.
